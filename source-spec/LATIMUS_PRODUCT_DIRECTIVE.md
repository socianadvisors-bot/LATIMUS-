# 12. SOURCE CONFIDENCE & EVIDENCE

Use four evidence tiers.

## Tier A — Authoritative

Government, regulatory, court, official filing or comparable primary record.

## Tier B — First-Party

Company website, investor relations, official press release, official careers page or equivalent.

## Tier C — Reputable Secondary

Established news/trade publication or comparable third-party source.

Material claims should be corroborated where practical.

## Tier D — Discovery Only

Aggregators, social posts, reviews, unverified databases and low-confidence secondary material.

Tier D cannot independently justify executive outreach.

For every signal, store separately:

- event date;
- publication date;
- date discovered;
- retrieval/import timestamp.

Also store:

- source;
- source ID;
- source URL/reference;
- source tier;
- evidence excerpt/structured facts;
- confidence;
- geography;
- event/project/transaction ID;
- relevant amount/value;
- observed vs inferred status;
- review status.

Do not send “congratulations on your expansion” based on a stale, cancelled or contradicted event.

# 15. ENTERPRISE OPPORTUNITY SCORE — EOS

Create a universal consulting-opportunity score separate from construction permit/project-specific scoring.

Maximum 100.

## Trigger Strength — 30

Recency, directness, materiality and verifiability.

## Demonstrated Need / Pain — 25

Evidence of operational, financial, transaction, project, technology, logistics, real-estate, energy, growth or management need.

## Economic Capacity — 20

Evidence of meaningful project size, company scale, funding, contract value, transaction size or economic significance.

## THE CONSULT Service Fit — 15

Strength of fit between the likely need and THE CONSULT capabilities.

## Decision-Maker Accessibility — 10

Ability to identify and professionally reach the outcome-owning function.

## Action Bands

- 90–100: Immediate Executive Pursuit
- 75–89: High-Priority Personalized Campaign
- 65–74: Qualified Outreach
- 50–64: Monitor / Nurture
- below 50: Intelligence Database Only

## POC Enrichment Gate

Default: meaningful POC/contact enrichment begins at **EOS 65+**.

Owner may override with documented reason.

Evidence confidence remains separate from EOS.

A 95 EOS with poor evidence must be reviewed/blocked.

Version the EOS formula and all overrides.
