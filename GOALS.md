# GOALS.md — LATIMUS

## Goal
Deliver LATIMUS as THE CONSULT's independent local-first Executive Office operating infrastructure with embedded cross-sector market/opportunity intelligence.

## Success conditions
LATIMUS independently runs executive/corporate operations, research and intelligence, qualified CRM/outreach, client commercial lifecycle, PMO/delivery, seven sector functions, M&A coordination, owner/family-enterprise support, approvals, evidence, reporting, offline/local workflows, and encrypted recovery.

The core loops are:
- `INTELLIGENCE -> DECISION -> EXECUTION -> MEASUREMENT -> LEARNING`
- `MARKET -> SIGNAL -> COMPANY -> NEED -> OPPORTUNITY -> POC -> OUTREACH -> RESULT`
- `DIAGNOSE -> STRUCTURE -> MOBILIZE -> EXECUTE -> MEASURE -> OPTIMIZE`

## Non-goals
- Do not build FORWARD in this repo.
- Do not require a hosted backend for the core product.
- Do not create 360 persistent agents.
- Do not claim always-on monitoring when the local browser cannot perform it.
- Do not fabricate sources, freshness, POCs, sends, credentials or professional authority.

Add the CSV-first Prospecting Workbench (import/mapping/normalization/company-first research/evidence/contact confidence/review/explicit CRM/export), and reconcile all55 master-plan sources in the existing Intelligence Fabric. Reuse the company/contact/evidence spine with distinct intake origins. Keep MV3 Advanced Mode and self-contained Portable Core; translate the standalone prospect spec into these runtimes, with no Electron or required backend. Keep every original CSV column and all three scoring systems separate.
