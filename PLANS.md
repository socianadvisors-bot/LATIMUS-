# PLANS.md — LATIMUS — Cross-Sector Executive Intelligence

This file is the roadmap. Detailed implementation/acceptance instructions live in `harness/build/phase-*.md`.

## Phase sequence
- `00-rebaseline` — **Re-baseline and product rename**: Preserve existing Executive AI/LATIMUS work, reconcile product identity, index requirements and establish evidence-backed status.
- `01-browser-foundation` — **Shared browser core, security and persistence**: Establish the two-distribution local-first platform.
- `02-durable-work-engine` — **Durable work engine and action safety**: Make long-running browser work resumable and auditable.
- `03-capabilities-ai` — **Capability registry and AI orchestration**: Turn the original Executive Office scope into callable, reviewable runtime capabilities.
- `04-intelligence-fabric` — **Intelligence Fabric foundation**: Build native market/opportunity intelligence inside LATIMUS.
- `05-commercial-loop` — **Qualified opportunity to engagement**: Connect intelligence to CRM, outreach, discovery, Blueprint, pricing and engagement.
- `06-delivery-loop` — **First complete client-delivery loop**: Prove LATIMUS can execute work after the sale.
- `07-executive-office-breadth` — **Complete Executive Office capability breadth**: Implement the remaining original sector, M&A, owner/family, corporate and assurance functions.
- `08-source-expansion` — **Cross-sector source expansion and cadence**: Expand the Intelligence Fabric across all signal families and research cadences.
- `09-integrations` — **Approved providers and business integrations**: Add direct browser-capable integrations without making them required for core operation.
- `10-learning-management` — **Learning, management intelligence and authority content**: Close the loop from outcomes to source yield, market strategy and controlled content.
- `11-hardening-release` — **Cross-platform hardening and release**: Prove the actual built distributions are safe, recoverable and usable.

## Plan rules
- Execute phases in dependency order unless the repository evidence supports a documented reordering.
- Within a phase, use vertical slices rather than building every screen first.
- Update context/build-log/traceability continuously.
- When context becomes large, externalize findings to harness files instead of restating the complete product directive.
- Never change the two-system separation contract as a convenience refactor.
